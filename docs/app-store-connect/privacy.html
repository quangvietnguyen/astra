Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

This policy explains how the Astra mobile application handles information. Astra is operated by the developer identified on its App Store listing. Questions can be sent to viet.ngquang92@gmail.com.

Information used for app functionality

Astra may request foreground location permission. If allowed, the operating system provides latitude, longitude, altitude, accuracy, and a best-effort place name. Astra uses this information while the app is running to adjust the lunar view for your perspective and to display location status. The app remains usable if permission is denied.

Astra does not operate a server that stores precise location. Precise coordinates and place names are not included in Astra's analytics events. Location and reverse-geocoding requests may be processed by your device's operating-system services under the platform provider's terms.

Analytics information

Astra uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand aggregate product usage and improve the experience. Analytics may automatically process a pseudonymous app-instance identifier, approximate geography derived from network information, device and operating-system information, language, app version, sessions, and basic diagnostic transport metadata.

Astra records interactions such as Moon-size selection, orbiter visibility, date exploration, and whether the telemetry panel is visible. These events do not include names, email addresses, precise GPS coordinates, city names, or a developer-defined user ID.

Advertising and tracking

Astra contains no advertising and does not use information for cross-app tracking. The iOS build excludes advertising-identifier support. Astra does not sell personal information or share information with data brokers.

Third-party processing

Google processes Analytics data on the developer's behalf. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Google Analytics data safeguards.

Retention and security

Analytics information is retained according to the configured Google Analytics property settings and only for as long as reasonably needed for measurement, security, and product improvement. Data is transmitted using standard encrypted network connections.

Your choices

  • Decline location permission when prompted.
  • Change Astra's location permission at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Delete the app to stop future collection from that installation.
  • Contact the developer with a privacy question or request.

Astra has no account system and does not receive information that can reliably identify a particular person in Analytics. This may limit the developer's ability to locate or delete a particular pseudonymous Analytics record.

Children

Astra is a general-audience educational utility and is not designed or marketed specifically to children under 13. It does not knowingly request a child's name, contact details, or account information.

International processing

Third-party service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live, subject to their contractual and legal safeguards.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Astra's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised, and material changes will be communicated as required.
