Astra Support

Need help with Astra? Email viet.ngquang92@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, Astra version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send precise location coordinates.

Current version

Astra 1.0.0 for iPhone and iPad

Location is unavailable

Astra works without location by using a default equatorial view. To enable your local perspective, open iOS Settings, find Astra, set Location to While Using the App, and reopen Astra.

The Moon view or controls look incorrect

Close and reopen Astra. If the issue continues, restart the device and include a screenshot in your support email. Avoid including your coordinates or other personal information.

The app icon changed

Astra can update its icon to match the current lunar phase or a supported lunar event. This is expected behavior.

Is the orbiter live NASA telemetry?

No. It is an artistic, educational visualization inspired by the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter. Astra is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.

Accounts, purchases, and deletion

Astra has no user accounts, subscriptions, purchases, or user-created cloud content. Deleting the app removes its local app data and stops future collection from that installation.

Privacy

Read the Astra Privacy Policy for details about optional location processing and Firebase Analytics.
