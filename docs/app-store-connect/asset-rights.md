5. A saved copy of the license or permission evidence.

## NASA reference

The app uses the factual name “NASA LRO” and presents an original 3D spacecraft visualization. Do not use the NASA insignia, logotype, or imagery without confirming the applicable media and identity-use rules. Keep the independent-app disclaimer in the product description and review notes.
