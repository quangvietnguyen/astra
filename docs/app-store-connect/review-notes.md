## Paste-ready review notes

Astra is an accountless educational Moon visualization for iPhone and iPad.

Testing path:
1. Launch the app. It requests foreground location access once. Location is optional; denying permission leaves the app usable with an equatorial default view.
2. The main scene displays a 3D Moon with calculated phase lighting.
3. On iPhone, swipe horizontally across the lower telemetry cards to view Moon metrics, Moon-size controls, the orbiter controls, and date controls. On iPad, all four cards are displayed together.
4. Use -1 Day, +1 Day, or the date presets to preview other lunar phases. Tap Today to reset.
5. Toggle ORBITER: ON/OFF to show or hide the LRO-inspired spacecraft.
6. Tap or swipe the lower handle to hide or restore the telemetry deck.

Foreground location is used to adjust the lunar view and display the device's coordinates and a best-effort place name. Astra does not send precise coordinates or city names in analytics events and does not operate a user-data backend.

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase for pseudonymous product-interaction analytics. Advertising-ID support is disabled. There are no advertisements and no cross-app tracking.

The “NASA LRO” presentation is an independent artistic and educational visualization inspired by the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter. It is not live mission telemetry, and Astra is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.

No demo account is required.
